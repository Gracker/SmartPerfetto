## Selected interval evidence

`scene_evidence.kind=selected_foundation`: product acquisition before your turn.
Read `native` and `metric` independently: captured/committed/partial are collection
states, not proof, complete coverage or a conclusion. Cite native
`evidenceRefId`/`rowIndex`/column and exact row value; metric `cells` follow `fields`
and reference `artifacts`. `captureRows`/`metricBinding` describe retained metadata,
not a verification verdict. Reuse these facts; `fetch_artifact` omitted cells and
acquire only missing evidence within the original identity/window. Missing
identity, unavailable data and partial coverage remain explicit gaps.
